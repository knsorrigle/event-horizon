# Credits and licences

Every third-party asset used by the site, with its source and licence.

## Audio

**No recorded audio is used.** Every sound is synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio
API (`src/audio/`), so the sound layer is copyright-free by construction:

| Sound | How it's made |
|---|---|
| Score bed | Sine/triangle drone on D1 with saturation and a band-limited saw harmonic layer; five detuned sawtooth pad voices gliding through a D Aeolian progression |
| Tape hiss, distant radio | Pink noise (Paul Kellet's filter), high-passed / band-passed and gated |
| Time-dilation pulse | Additive bell (inharmonic partial stack) |
| Reverb | Generated impulse response (decaying, darkening noise) |
| Scroll rush, rumble | Pink and brown noise, filtered |
| Body pings and hum | Additive sines on a Dm11 chord |
| Slingshot whoosh | Noise sweep, pitch glide and low impact, rendered once in an `OfflineAudioContext` (played reversed for "back to orbit") |
| White hole, ticks, toggle tone | Filtered noise bursts and sines |

**Optional recorded layer.** `audioParams.ambientTrack.url` (in `src/audio/params.ts`) can point at a
recorded ambient bed to mix under the generative score. It is unset. Any file added there must be
**CC0 or carry an explicit free licence that allows use on a website without restriction**, and must
be listed here with its source URL and licence before it ships.
